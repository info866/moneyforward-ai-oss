# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific when reviewing code or reports. Discuss observable
behavior, not personal characteristics. Do not harass, threaten or publish
another person's private information. Assume mistakes can be corrected and
provide a minimal, safe reproduction. Maintainers may remove abusive or
privacy-invasive content and restrict participation to protect the project.
For a sensitive report, use the private process in SECURITY.md; never include
private financial or employee data in a public discussion.
